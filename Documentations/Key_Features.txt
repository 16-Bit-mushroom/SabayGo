Key features of a ride-sharing app

[ system shared ]
	Two-way rating and review system
		- After each trip, both the rider and the driver should be prompted to leave a rating and, if they choose, a short review.
	Clear fare estimation
		- Before a rider confirms their booking, your app should provide a clear and accurate estimate of the fare. This calculation should consider the distance and estimated time.
		
	

[ Passenger App ] 
	Account Registration and Profile Creation
		- history of activities
		- store important information such payment data, personal information,
		- manage profile settings
		- history of rides
		- user rating
		- various registration methods to speed up the registration process.
	Notifications
		- inform users in real time about accepted, upcoming, or canceled rides
		- send important information like messages to drivers
	Geo-location and Routing
		- access to an integrated map to select the needed destination and correctly mark their location for the pickup.
		- monitoring their ride and its current location in real time,
		- Users expect to see exactly where their driver is and how long it will take for them to arrive.
	Payment Options
		-
	Messages and Call
		- helps passengers and drivers communicate either via text messages or calls
		- 
	
	

[ Driver App ] 
	Profile and Registration
		- same as for the passenger app but should also include a driver’s license number and other important information related to his work (i.e., might be compliance with local regulations).
		- For drivers, it’s even more critical. Their profile should display their vehicle information, a photo and their overall rating. This transparency is fundamental for safety and trust on both sides.
		-
	Request Management
		- allows drivers to manage requests such as accepting or declining them as well as viewing. 
	Trip Data
		- display information about the upcoming trip, such as precise pickup location, information about the passenger, and expected duration of the trip.
	Geo-location and Routing
		- same as for passengers, this feature is critical for drivers as it lets them navigate, correctly identify pickup locations, and create faster and more secure routes. 
	Payment Receive
		- drivers should be able to monitor their income, access payment history, and receive payments
		helps notify drivers about important information (like an upcoming pickup) and get in touch with the riders via the preferred communication method.
	Notifications & Messaging
	Availability toggle 
		– Let drivers mark themselves online or offline as per their availability.
	
Admin App:
	Examples of features that an admin app might have:
	    	Customer service and 24/7 support
	    	Tracking of rides and of drivers’ activity
	    	Enrollment process for drivers
	    	Payment management system and accounting
